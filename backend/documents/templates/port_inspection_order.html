II. Request for Action

Pursuant to FAO Port State Measures Agreement, Article 9(4), the Government of Ecuador, through the Galápagos National Park Directorate, formally REQUESTS that the {{ port.name | default('Port of Manta') }} authority undertake the following:

  1. Item 1: DENY PORT ENTRY to the subject vessel pending compliance investigation per PSMA Article 9(4);
  2. Item 2: If port entry is granted on humanitarian, safety, or compliance grounds, conduct a FULL INSPECTION of the vessel's logbook, fishing gear, catch, and AIS records per PSMA Article 11;
  3. Item 3: Detain any catch landed pending verification of legal origin per PSMA Article 18;
  4. Item 4: Furnish a written inspection report to the Galápagos National Park Directorate within seven (7) days of vessel arrival, addressed to the Marine Enforcement Unit;
  5. Item 5: Notify the IATTC Secretariat of the inspection outcome per IATTC Resolution C-19-01 §6.
